Build something real before sunrise.

VIBRONIX is a 12-hour offline hackathon hosted by the CSI Club of ICET. Teams gather on campus on the evening of 8 January and code through the night until 6:00 AM on 9 January.

Between the commits there are games, entertainment activities and food for everyone, so the night stays fun as well as focused.

  • Open to engineering students from all departments
  • Students from any college can join
  • Cross-college and cross-department teams are allowed
  • Teams of 3 to 4 members
  • Food, games and entertainment provided

What every participant takes home

Up to 20KTU activity points

Awarded to every participant of the event.

Certificate

A participation certificate for each team member.

₹20,000

Total prize pool for the winning teams.

How to take part

Seats are limited, so participation is by shortlisting. Only shortlisted teams can attend the hackathon.

  1. Register your team

    Fill in the registration form with the details of all 3 or 4 members. It is free.

  2. Submit your abstract

    Add your project abstract in the same form. This is what we shortlist on.

  3. Get shortlisted

    The CSI ICET team reviews every entry and contacts the shortlisted teams directly.

  4. Hack through the night

    Arrive at ICET on 8 January at 6:00 PM and build until 6:00 AM.
